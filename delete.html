Delete Account & Data

On-device data

All files, edits and settings live only on your device. To delete them: uninstall the app, or use Settings → Apps → All PDF Editor → Clear data.

Google account data

If you used Word↔PDF conversion, temporary Drive files are auto-deleted after each conversion. To revoke access: Google Account → Third-party access → remove All PDF Editor.

Request deletion

Email support@yourdomain.com with subject "Delete my data" and we will confirm within 30 days.
