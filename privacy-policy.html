Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QR Scanner ("we", "our") handles information when you use the app.

1. Data stored on your device

All scanned codes, created codes, favorites and settings are stored only on your device in the app's private storage. We do not collect, upload, or sell this data.

2. Camera

The camera permission is used only to scan QR codes and barcodes in real time. Camera frames are processed on-device and are never transmitted anywhere.

3. Photos

When you use "Scan from gallery", the app accesses only the single photo you select. It is used only to decode a code and is never uploaded.

4. Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Google handles all payment information; we only receive your subscription status.

5. Internet usage

Internet is used only to: open links you choose to open, display this Privacy Policy / Terms & Conditions pages, and validate purchases.

6. Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

8. Contact us

Questions? Email support@qrexplore.com.
