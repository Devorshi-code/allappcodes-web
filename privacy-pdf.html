Privacy Policy

All PDF Editor

Last updated: 06 Aug 2026

1. Data We Process

All PDF Editor processes your documents locally on your device. Your files are never uploaded to our servers. Google Drive conversion (Word↔PDF) uses your own Google account; temporary files are deleted from your Drive immediately after conversion.

2. Permissions

  • Storage – to list and open documents on your device.
  • Camera – to scan documents.
  • Internet – for Google Drive conversion and ads.
  • Notifications – to inform you about finished operations (opt-in).

3. Third-Party Services

We use Google Play Services (sign-in/Drive) and ad networks that may collect device identifiers per their own policies.

4. Data Retention

Files you import stay on your device. Deleted files are kept in the in-app Recycle Bin until you remove them.

5. Your Rights

You may delete all app data via Delete Account & Data or by uninstalling the app.
