Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 01, 2026

Welcome to AI Image Generator ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your data. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights when using our mobile application.

🔒 TL;DR: We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data. Your prompts and generated images are processed by third-party AI services and stored only on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Our app does not require user accounts, email addresses, phone numbers, names, or any other personally identifiable information.

The only data processed by the app includes:

  • Prompts: Text descriptions you enter to generate images.
  • Reference Images: Images you optionally upload to guide AI generation.
  • Generation History: A local log of your generated images stored only on your device.

2. Third-Party Services

To provide AI image generation functionality, our app uses the following third-party services. When you generate an image, your prompt and (optionally) reference images are sent to these services:

🖼️ Pollinations.ai

Used to generate AI images from your text prompts. Your prompts and selected parameters (model, aspect ratio, style, seed) are sent to their servers to produce images.

Privacy Policy: https://pollinations.ai/privacy

📤 Catbox.moe

Used only when you upload reference images. Your uploaded images are temporarily hosted on their servers so the AI can use them as visual references.

Privacy Policy: https://catbox.moe/privacy.php

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third-party services, as they have their own data handling practices that are independent of ours.

3. Local Data Storage

All generation history (prompts, image URLs, timestamps) is stored locally on your device using an SQLite database. This data:

  • Never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it.
  • Is not synced to any cloud server or external database.
  • Can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

4. Permissions Required

Our app requests the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET: Required to communicate with Pollinations.ai and Catbox.moe for image generation and reference image uploads.
  • STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES: Required to let you select reference images from your gallery and to save generated images to your device.

We do not access your contacts, location, microphone, camera, or any other sensitive data.

5. Analytics & Advertising

Our app does NOT:

  • Use any analytics or tracking SDKs (no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, etc.).
  • Display advertisements.
  • Collect device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, advertising ID).
  • Share any data with advertisers or marketing partners.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

7. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal data on external servers, there is minimal risk of data breaches. All communications with third-party services are conducted over secure HTTPS connections.

8. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • You can delete individual generated images from within the app at any time.
  • You can clear all app data through your device's Settings → Apps → AI Image Generator → Clear Data.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time, which removes all locally stored data.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Effective Date" at the top. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
